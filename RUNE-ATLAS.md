# Rune atlas — 98 mixed-element spells

Every pair of the seven elements now has three patterns: a two-gem outer crescent and two three-gem patterns. Every set of three different elements has an additional spell. Alongside your class spells and simple captured runes, the full atlas contains 112 patterns for each class.

Recipes become known when your campaign deck contains enough copies of every required gem. One new foreign gem opens combinations with both starting elements; a second copy unlocks patterns requiring two of it. Existing decks gain their combinations without a reset.

Weaves attune to a starting-class element in their pattern when possible. The in-game spell row shows the resulting school and live effect: Water builds Tide; Light spends Tide for +2 damage/healing or +1 card per Tide; Fire builds Heat and adds it to attacks; Air vents Heat into healing; Dark attacks steal life for Nightbinder, while Blood damage/draw weaves cost that class 2 HP. A weave without a class element still works, but does not trigger that class trait.

Use **Weaves** to browse combinations, **Ready** for recipes you can cast now, and **Match** for patterns compatible with the gems already on the board. Number keys follow the visible list. In the spellbook, **All runes** includes future recipes and shows the missing gems.

## Example: Tidecaller captures Dark

One Dark adds Hex plus five weaves: Blackwater (Water + Dark), Drowned Memory (2 Water + Dark), Eclipse Spark (Light + Dark), Twilight Pact (2 Light + Dark), and Dusk Covenant (Water + Light + Dark). Blackwater builds Tide; Eclipse Spark spends it. A second Dark adds Undertow and Duskfire.

Adding Earth afterward also creates Earth/Dark patterns and new three-element combinations. Rewards show the exact number of newly craftable patterns and example names before you choose.
